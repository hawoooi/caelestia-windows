What each knob actually costs

Breathing. B changes nothing but the padding, so it is the honest control: if B already looks right, the bar was never too thin, it was too crowded. The times sat 4px from the track, which at 11px type reads as touching. 16px either side is the same rhythm the rest of the window uses.

Radius. A pill radius is half the thickness, and it is what makes a thick bar read as a lozenge rather than a track. Dropping to 2–3px keeps the corner from looking cut while letting the bar stay rectangular; 0 (case D) is the sharpest and sits closest to the square corners on the transport glyphs. Note the filled part inherits the radius, so at 0 the play head is a hard vertical edge — which is either precise or blunt depending on taste.

Thickness. Past about 14px the bar stops reading as a line and starts reading as a container, which is what makes F work: once it is a container, putting the times inside it is natural rather than crowded, and the seekbar reclaims the ~90px the two clocks were spending. E is the same slab without that move, for comparison.

G and H are the risky ones. G's marker gives an unambiguous play head at any thickness, but adds a moving part. H trades exact position for the same stepped texture the cava visualiser has elsewhere in this theme — it is the most characterful and the least precise, since a 40-cell bar quantises a 3-minute track to about 4.5 seconds per cell.

Recommendation

C for the safe pick — thick enough to grab, 3px corners, and the padding fixed. F if you want the top bar to feel deliberately designed rather than merely tidy: it is the only option that gets the seekbar more width instead of less.
