Visualiser — nine designs

Every card is the real 300×64 it occupies under the library, on the real surface. All nine read the same signal, so differences you see are differences in the design and not in the input. Form and colour are independent — pick a number and a letter.

Ramp
Level

The parameter that decides all of them

Attack fast, decay slow. A meter that rises and falls at the same rate looks like noise; one that snaps up and sinks back looks like music. Every design here shares one envelope — rise 55% of the remaining distance per frame, fall 12% — and that single asymmetry does more for how right this feels than any choice of shape. If a design ever looks frantic, check this before redrawing anything.

V2's cap is the same idea made explicit: it holds the maximum and falls at a constant rate rather than a proportional one, so it descends in a straight line instead of easing. That difference is what makes a peak marker legible rather than mushy.

What 64px costs

The card is 40px of drawable height after padding. That is generous for bars and fine for a curve, but it is the reason there is no radial design here and why V9's frequency axis is coarse — 40 rows is not much to divide a spectrum across. It also means tall thin bars are out: below about 4px wide the bar stops reading as a bar and starts aliasing, which is why V8 goes all the way to 1px and becomes texture instead of trying to be a small chart.

Colour, and the one accent problem

The palette has eight roles and a single accent. cava's own theme file on this desktop sweeps primary → tertiary → error, a hue walk from calm floor to hot peak — and neither tertiary nor error exists in theme.css. So none of these can reproduce that ramp today. The three offered instead:

 A muted — outline → primary. Most of a bar's area is its lower half, so putting the quiet tone there sets the overall level and the accent only appears at the tips.
 B accent — flat primary. Brightest; competes with the seekbar, which is the only other accent-filled thing on screen.
 C no accent — greyscale. The visualiser becomes pure texture and spends none of the theme's one accent on ambient decoration.

If you want the real cava gradient, that is a palette change rather than a design change: add tertiary and error to palette.json and to render-theme-css.py / render-theme-js.py, and every one of these gets it for free.

What each costs to build for real

The panel draws with GDI through Spider Monkey Panel, so rectangles are cheap and curves are not. V1, V2, V3, V4, V7 and V8 are all FillSolidRect loops — trivial, and the gradient is done by picking a colour per bar rather than per pixel. V5, V6 and V9 need per-pixel work: V5 and V6 want a path, and V9 needs a scrolling bitmap it owns and blits each frame. All three are possible, but they are the ones that could reintroduce the paint cost that made scrolling laggy earlier in this project.

Where the audio comes from is still unproven. The WebView host exposes a shared PCM buffer; SMP has no audio API of its own. That has not been tested, and until it is, treat every one of these as a design decision rather than a scheduled feature.

Recommendation

V8 Hairline with ramp C if you want it to disappear into the theme — it reads as a texture on the card rather than a gadget bolted to it, and it is the only one that never competes with the seekbar for attention. V5 Ridge with ramp A if you want it to be a deliberate feature: it is the most elegant of the nine and the only one with no flicker at all, because nothing about it is discrete.

V4 Segmented has an argument the others do not: cava is already running on this desktop with exactly that stepped texture, so choosing it makes the player agree with the rest of the environment rather than merely coexist with it. That is a real reason, and it would be my pick if consistency matters more than restraint.

I would avoid V2 and V9 here. V2's caps are hi-fi-equipment styling that this theme has spent a lot of effort not having, and V9 is the most interesting of the nine but wants three times the height to be readable — at 40px it is a smear, and its information is wasted.
